Long-form and Shorts · YouTube2026

DeadTell TV

A behavioural-analysis channel that dramatises everyday psychological manipulation through a surveillance-camera aesthetic, then breaks down the moment the mask slips.

Format
8–9-minute episodes in three acts with two ad breaks, cut down into 30–40-second Shorts.
My role
Showrunner: topic research, script, performance prompts, generation and edit.
Craft
Sustained acting performance from synthetic people, with a randomised casting profile so the image model does not fall back on narrow racial defaults.
Learned
Kling 3.0 gave the most believable acting against Seedance 2.0 and Veo 3.1: 4.3 generations per usable shot, US$12–18 per finished video.

Long-form ambient · YouTube2025–now

Terra Atavica

Public-domain historical artwork, colourised and paired with original AI-composed music: rooms you can sit in from worlds that no longer exist.

Format
2–3-hour ambient videos for study, focus and sleep, in five musical movements.
My role
Creative director: theme, image selection, music direction and the final timeline in DaVinci Resolve.
Craft
Every image is screened for content and historical context before generation; era-accurate skin-tone instructions stop colourisation whitewashing people.

More than half of archive animations were usable first pass, but some altered subject identity, object behaviour or skin tone. Authenticity approval stays human.

AI micro-drama · R&DJul 2026

Youniverse

One self-portrait in; a short drama about a life the person didn't live out. Three characters, one world and its props have to stay consistent across a sequence of generated shots.

Format

Evaluation

The notes I gave on broadcast cuts, written as rubrics and labels that another rater, or a model, can apply. The full studies, with method, findings and limits, are on a separate page.

Text-to-video rubric

2024–25

Six Yes/No dimensions (visual realism, motion coherence, temporal consistency, creative originality, prompt adherence, shot success) applied to three commercial text-to-video models at a Singapore government agency. Scores of 7, 15 and 11 out of 18, with prompt faults written up apart from model faults.

Side-by-side model tests

2025–26

Run on my own productions against a written definition of a usable shot: Kling, Seedance and Veo for acting; Grok Imagine and Sora for a consistent character; Kling and Grok Imagine for fidelity to archive artwork.

Failure labels & golden set

In progress

Five failure classes (tool, brief, performance, identity and continuity, taste) and a 163-item golden set of keyframes and takes, to check new model versions against my verdicts. Built November 2026; labels not yet confirmed.
